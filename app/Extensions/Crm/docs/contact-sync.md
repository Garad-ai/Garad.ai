# Contact Sync

Bring contacts from your other tools into the CRM — automatically, or all at once.

## What it does

When someone new is captured in MarketingBot or your Chatbot, their details can be copied into your CRM Contacts so everything lives in one place. No more re-typing contacts by hand.

Contacts can come from:

- **WhatsApp (MarketingBot)** — contacts you add or import.
- **Telegram (MarketingBot)** — people who join your groups.
- **Chatbot** — visitors who share their email in a chat.

Each synced contact is added to your CRM as **Active** and tagged with where it came from.

## Turning it on

Go to **Settings → CRM → Contact Sync** and switch on:

1. **Enable Contact Sync** — the master switch. Nothing syncs unless this is on.
2. The source(s) you want: **WhatsApp**, **Telegram**, and/or **Chatbot Customers**.

A contact only syncs when both the master switch and that source are enabled. Everything is off by default, so you choose exactly what flows into the CRM.

## Automatic sync

With the switches on, new contacts appear in your CRM on their own as they come in — a new WhatsApp contact, a new Telegram member, or a chatbot visitor who leaves their email.

## Sync everything at once

Already have contacts from before? On each contact list page (WhatsApp, Telegram, Chatbot) you'll see a **Sync to CRM** button. Click it and a window shows a live progress bar while your existing contacts are copied into the CRM, with a running count of how many were added or updated. You can run it again any time — it won't create duplicates.

## No duplicates

Sync is smart about matches:

- If a contact's **email** already exists in your CRM, it updates that contact instead of adding a new one.
- If there's no email, it matches on **phone number**.
- It only fills in blank fields — anything you've already edited in the CRM is left untouched.

So you can sync as often as you like without messing up your data.

## Good to know

- Chatbot visitors are only synced once they've shared an email.
- Names are split into first and last name automatically.
- The **Sync to CRM** buttons only show up when contact sync is switched on for that source.
